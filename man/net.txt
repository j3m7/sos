NAME
    net - Wi-Fi link state, address and ARP counters

SYNOPSIS
    net

DESCRIPTION
    The Wi-Fi link state and signal strength (RSSI), the IP address, gateway
    and hostname, and the ARP packets received and sent with the keepalive
    state. While connected the board announces its address once a minute (a
    gratuitous ARP), without which a device that sends nothing stops receiving
    broadcasts and clients get "no route to host" once the router forgets it;
    'wifi keepalive off' turns the announcement off until reboot, for testing.

SEE ALSO
    wifi, hostname, ssh
