NAME
    chat - talk with the others in the chat room

SYNOPSIS
    chat

DESCRIPTION
    Joins the chat room: shows the last few lines and who is there, then each
    line typed at the 'chat> ' prompt goes to everyone else in the room, as

        [14:03] alice@laptop: hi

    Joining and leaving are announced. In the room:

        /who          who is in the room
        /quit         leave (so do Ctrl-C, and Ctrl-D on an empty line)
        //text        send a line that begins with '/'

    Tab completes the word being typed: /who, /quit, or the name of someone in
    the room (a name with a space in it isn't offered).

    Messages are kept only in memory (the last 16), never in the log or on the
    card. See 'wall' for how messages arrive and what happens to a reader who
    falls behind.

SEE ALSO
    wall, who
