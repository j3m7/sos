NAME
    log - show or set the log levels

SYNOPSIS
    log
    log console <level>      (console only)
    log history <level>      (console only)

DESCRIPTION
    Two levels decide what the log does with a message: 'console' is what the
    serial console prints as it happens (default notice), 'history' what is
    kept in memory for 'dmesg' (default info). Levels, from most to least
    important: err, warning, notice, info, debug. A change lasts until reboot
    and is itself logged (facility auth).

SEE ALSO
    dmesg, logger
