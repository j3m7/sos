NAME
    head - the first lines of a command's output, then stop it

SYNOPSIS
    <command> | head [<n>]

DESCRIPTION
    Passes on the first n lines of the command's output (10 without a
    number; 1 to 999999) and drops the rest. Once it has them the command is
    stopped at once, as Ctrl-C would stop it but without '^C': 'cat big.log |
    head 3' doesn't read the rest of the file. A command that isn't a job
    (dmesg, help) runs to its end, its further output dropped.

    Stages after it still get their turn: 'dmesg | head 5 | wc' counts the
    five lines.

NOTES
    head works only after a '|': typed on its own it says so. More about
    pipes in 'man more'.

SEE ALSO
    tail, grep, wc, more
