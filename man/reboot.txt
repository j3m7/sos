NAME
    reboot - restart the device

SYNOPSIS
    reboot

DESCRIPTION
    Restarts the board at once. The configuration is in flash and the clock
    comes back from the RTC, so nothing is lost but SSH sessions, running jobs
    and the chat. A reboot doesn't count as a watchdog or fault reset in
    'uptime'. After a crash or watchdog reset, '/autorun.lua' is skipped until
    the next normal boot, which a reboot is.
