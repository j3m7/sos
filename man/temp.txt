NAME
    temp - the chip's temperature, and the lowest and highest since boot

SYNOPSIS
    temp
    temp cal [<degrees> | off]          (change: console only)

DESCRIPTION
    The RP2350's own temperature sensor, read every 10 s, with the lowest and
    highest readings since boot and how long ago each was taken. It is logged
    with the supply voltage (see 'power'): at boot, when it has moved a degree
    or the supply 0.2 V (at most once a minute), when USB power comes or goes,
    and at least once an hour.

    It is the chip's temperature, not the room's: the chip runs warmer than
    the air, more so under Wi-Fi load. Uncalibrated, the sensor can be a few
    degrees off, but it is good for change over time on one board. The last
    line of 'temp' says whether a calibration is set. The glitch detector's
    report ('glitch') includes the last reading before a detector reset.

    'temp cal <degrees>' calibrates it: give what a thermometer beside the
    board reads now, in degrees C (e.g. 23.5; -40 to 125). The difference
    from the sensor's own reading, at most 15 degrees either way, is saved and
    added to every reading after: 'temp', the log and the glitch detector's
    reports. 'temp cal' alone shows it; 'temp cal off' removes it, and so does
    'config reset' ('temp_cal' in a provisioning file sets it).

    To calibrate: leave the board unpowered beside the thermometer for half an
    hour, power it up, and within a minute of Wi-Fi connecting run 'temp cal'
    with the thermometer's reading. The chip is already a little warmer than
    the room by then, so a calibrated reading is closer to the room's
    temperature as seen from the chip than to the chip's own: don't compare it
    with the chip's rated maximum. It corrects the offset only, so it is best
    near the temperature it was set at.
