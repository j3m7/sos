NAME
    wc - count the lines, words and bytes of a command's output

SYNOPSIS
    <command> | wc

DESCRIPTION
    Prints, once the command has finished, three numbers: the newlines, the
    words and the bytes of its output ('help | wc', 'ls | wc'), counted as
    Unix wc counts them: a word is a run of anything but spaces, tabs and
    line breaks, and bytes are bytes, not characters. Ctrl-C while the command
    runs drops the count.

NOTES
    wc works only after a '|': typed on its own it says so. More about pipes
    in 'man more'.
