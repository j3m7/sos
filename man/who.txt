NAME
    who - who is logged in, and who is in the chat

SYNOPSIS
    who

DESCRIPTION
    One line per shell: its slot (0 is the console), the person's name, where
    they are connected from, 'chat' if they are in the chat room, and how long
    they have been logged in. Yours is marked '*'.

    A person is named after the comment of the trusted key they logged in
    with ('alice@laptop'; 'key <n>' if it has none), the console is 'console',
    and a second login with the same key gets its slot added ('alice@laptop/2').

SEE ALSO
    wall, chat, ssh
