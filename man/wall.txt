NAME
    wall - send a message to every other shell

SYNOPSIS
    wall <message>

DESCRIPTION
    Shows the message in every other shell at once, as

        [wall 14:02 UTC alice@laptop] message

    and says how many shells it was sent to. Times are UTC and say so, or time
    since boot ('+1h02m') until the clock is set. A message arrives between the
    reader's keystrokes: their line is cleared, the message printed, and the
    prompt and their typing redrawn; during a running command it appears on its
    own line. A full-screen program (edit, more, man) holds messages until it
    quits.

    Messages are kept only in memory (the last 16), never in the log or on the
    card. A reader whose client has stopped reading doesn't hold the sender up
    for more than 2 seconds; after that it misses messages and is told how many.

    Quote a message that holds an apostrophe, a '|' or '>' or repeated spaces:
    wall "don't reboot" (QUOTING in 'man intro').
