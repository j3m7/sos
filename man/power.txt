NAME
    power - the supply voltage, and whether USB power is present

SYNOPSIS
    power

DESCRIPTION
    The supply voltage (VSYS, the board's input) and whether USB power is
    present, with the lowest and highest since boot and how long ago each was.
    On USB power VSYS is the USB voltage less a diode's drop (about 0.35 V); a
    battery or other supply feeds it directly. Within a few percent. A reading
    taken during a Wi-Fi transmission catches its dip (about 0.13 V on USB
    power), so the lowest can be a brief one. Read every 10 s and logged with
    the temperature (see 'temp'); the glitch detector's report includes the
    last reading before a detector reset.
