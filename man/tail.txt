NAME
    tail - the last lines of a command's output

SYNOPSIS
    <command> | tail [<n>]

DESCRIPTION
    Shows the last n lines of the command's output (10 without a number; 1 to
    999999) once the command has finished ('ls | tail 3', 'dmesg | tail').
    It keeps at most 4 KB: when the lines asked for are longer than that, it
    shows the last whole lines that fit, and says nothing about it. A single
    line longer than 4 KB shows its end. A last line without a newline is
    counted as a line, as it came.

    Ctrl-C while the command runs drops what tail holds: nothing is shown.

NOTES
    tail works only after a '|': typed on its own it says so. More about
    pipes in 'man more'.
