NAME
    logger - add a line to the system log

SYNOPSIS
    logger [-p <level>] <message>

DESCRIPTION
    Adds the message to the log under facility 'user', at level 'notice'
    unless -p gives err, warning, notice, info or debug. The line says which
    shell it came from: 'logger (console): ...' or 'logger (ssh <name>): ...',
    the name as 'who' shows it. At most 10 a second; beyond that it says the
    message wasn't logged. Lua scripts log the same way with sos.log().

    Quote a message that holds an apostrophe, a '|' or '>' (an '->' too) or
    repeated spaces: logger "it's 40 -> 41" (QUOTING in 'man intro').

SEE ALSO
    dmesg, log, who
